Why history is not a prediction

A high or low result in a previous round is not reliable evidence that the next round will be high or low. A hash function also does not decrypt a secret seed. If a game exposes a verifiable fairness protocol, the correct seed and round data can help verify completed rounds—but that is different from knowing an unrevealed future outcome.
